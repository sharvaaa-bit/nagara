The problem and our solution

One platform for every city service.

The problem

City services are scattered across offices, websites and phone lines. Citizens waste time finding information, cannot reach help quickly, and never know if a reported problem was fixed.

The solution

NAGARA CityPulse brings transport, water and power, waste, citizen services and emergency help into one platform. Find information, use services, call for help and track civic issues from one screen on any device.
